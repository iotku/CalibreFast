Books on this device

Books you read are kept in this browser, so they open at once and can be read with no connection. These settings are for this device only.

How books are kept
  • Reading: kept from the time you open them until they go unopened for the time chosen above. Your most recent ones stay however long you're away.
  • On deck and Saved: if you choose to download them ahead of time, the first books of the home page's "On deck / Saved for later" row: the next book of each series you finished one of, then the books you saved longest ago. Saving more books doesn't change which; when one leaves the row, the next is downloaded.
  • Finished: kept for a day after you last open them, in case you want to go back to something.
  • Not Reading: a book you set to Not Reading is removed from this device right away, even one you chose to always keep.
  • Always keep (on a book's page): kept for good, whatever its size. The book is added to Saved if it isn't on any of your lists, so you can always find it.
  • Running out of room: when books would go over the storage limit, Finished books go first, then Saved books you haven't opened here (saved most recently first), then other books you're not reading (opened longest ago first). Books you're reading and books you chose to always keep are never removed to make room. Unless you choose a limit, it's half of what the browser lets this site store.
