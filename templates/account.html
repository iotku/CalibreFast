What's sent, and what isn't
  • Nothing about a book is sent until you share it{{ if .Share.All }} (you share every book){{ end }}: not its status, and not even its ISBN to find it on Hardcover.
  • For a shared book: Want to read, Reading and Finished become the same statuses on Hardcover, as soon as you set them here or start or finish it in a reader.
  • Only if you chose them above: how far along you are (as pages, a few minutes after you stop turning them, one page short of the end), and the dates you started and finished.
  • Never sent: your other favorites, shelves, highlights, notes, bookmarks, other books, or anything about other people here.
  • Nothing is ever deleted on Hardcover. Clearing a status, or no longer sharing a book, leaves what was sent there; change it on Hardcover if you want it gone.
  • Nothing on Hardcover is copied here, except the authors you follow, when you ask for them below.
  • Authors you star here are followed on Hardcover, if you turn that on below. Taking a star off unfollows the author there only if you leave Also unfollow on Hardcover ticked when asked.
  • It appears on Hardcover as if you'd done it there, so your Hardcover privacy settings decide who sees it.

Track books you read here on your Hardcover account: whether you want to read, are reading or have finished them, and, if you choose, how far along you are and when you started and finished.

  1. Choose Link Hardcover. A short code appears here.
  2. Open hardcover.app/link, sign in to Hardcover, enter the code and approve CalibreFast.
  3. Choose which books to share: turn on Sync to Hardcover on a book's page. Or, here, choose to share every book.

Linking shares nothing by itself: no book is sent until you choose it. You need a Hardcover account (it's free). You never give CalibreFast your Hardcover password, and you can unlink at any time.

What you allow: CalibreFast may see who you are on Hardcover, read and change your Hardcover library, and see and change who you follow (only when you ask: to bring in the authors you follow, to follow those you star here if you turn that on, and to unfollow one when you take its star off and say so). It keeps that sign-in encrypted on this server, but whoever runs this server could use it, so link only if you trust them. Unlinking ends it.

Delete account

Delete your account and everything in it

Deleting your account signs you out everywhere, and in a week deletes for good:

  • your favorites, reading statuses, progress, bookmarks, highlights and notes;
  • your bookshelves and preferences;
  • your sync key (KOReader stops syncing) and linked sign-ins;
  • your link to Hardcover, if any (what was sent there stays there).

Books in the library aren't affected. Until {{ .DeletePurgeDay }}, an admin here can restore your account with everything in it, if you ask them; you can't sign in to it meanwhile. After that it can't be undone.
