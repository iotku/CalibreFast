Only admins see this. No token or secret is needed. -no-hardcover-sync hides it.

What's sent, and what isn't
  • Nothing about a book is sent until you share it{{ if .Share.All }} (you share every book){{ end }}: not its status, and not even its ISBN to find it on Hardcover.
  • For a shared book: Want to read, Reading and Finished become the same statuses on Hardcover, as soon as you set them here or start or finish it in a reader.
  • Only if you chose them above: how far along you are (as pages, a few minutes after you stop turning them, one page short of the end), and the dates you started and finished.
  • Never sent: your favorites, shelves, highlights, notes, bookmarks, other books, or anything about other people here.
  • Nothing is ever deleted on Hardcover, and nothing there is copied back here. Clearing a status, or no longer sharing a book, leaves what was sent there; change it on Hardcover if you want it gone.
  • It appears on Hardcover as if you'd done it there, so your Hardcover privacy settings decide who sees it.

Track books you read here on your Hardcover account: whether you want to read, are reading or have finished them, and, if you choose, how far along you are and when you started and finished.

  1. Choose Link Hardcover. A short code appears here.
  2. Open hardcover.app/link, sign in to Hardcover, enter the code and approve CalibreFast.
  3. Choose which books to share: tick Track on Hardcover on a book's page. Or, here, choose to share every book.

Linking shares nothing by itself: no book is sent until you choose it. You need a Hardcover account (it's free). You never give CalibreFast your Hardcover password, and you can unlink at any time.

What you allow: CalibreFast may see who you are on Hardcover, and read and change your Hardcover library. It keeps that sign-in encrypted on this server, but whoever runs this server could use it, so link only if you trust them. Unlinking ends it.
