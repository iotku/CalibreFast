Recycling Bin

Books removed from the library wait here with their files, and with a copy of metadata.db from just before each removal. Restore puts a book back as it was; anything else changed in the library since is kept.

The bin can't be emptied from here, so a stolen admin account can't remove the backups too. The server's owner empties it on the server: -empty-recycle-bin 30d deletes what was removed more than 30 days ago, or delete folders in the bin's folder.
