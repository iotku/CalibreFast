Privacy notice

This is a self-hosted, non-commercial service. It is free to use: nobody is charged for it, and it earns no money from advertising or from your information.

What we keep

  • Your account: your username and, if you set one, your password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Sign in with GitHub or Google: if you choose this, we receive your account's ID, email address and display name or username from that provider. We keep them to recognise you when you come back and to show you which account is linked. We ask for no other access, and we don't post, read your files or contacts, or use your account for anything else. The access token the provider gives us is used once, during sign-in, and isn't stored.
  • Your use of the service: what you choose to save here, such as favorites, progress, bookmarks, notes, collections, preferences, and device-sync and notification settings.
  • Sessions: when you sign in, a cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. A short-lived cookie is also used while you sign in with GitHub or Google. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs: like any web server, this one may log technical details of requests, such as IP addresses and times, to keep the service running and secure.

How it's used

Only to run the service for you: signing you in, keeping your settings and what you saved, and keeping the service secure. Your information isn't sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else. The exceptions are the sign-in provider you choose to use, and any other service you choose to link yourself, which receives only what that link is for. The administrator can manage accounts on this server.

Your choices

  • You can unlink GitHub or Google, change your password, and revoke device-sync keys on the Device and Hardcover sync page.
  • You can delete your account from your account page. It signs you out everywhere, and your data is removed for good after a short grace period in which the administrator can restore it if you ask.
  • To ask what is held about you, or to have something corrected or removed, contact this server's administrator.

Changes

This notice may be updated from time to time.
