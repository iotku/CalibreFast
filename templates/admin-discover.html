Tag rows on the home page

The home page shows a few “More in …” rows, picked from the tags that have one. Tags on a large share of the library (“Fiction”, “Ebook”) are left out, as are tags with very few books; of the rest, the biggest have rows. Include gives a tag a row whatever its size; Exclude keeps it off the home page.
